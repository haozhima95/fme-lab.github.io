<section class="page-hero"><div class="container"><p class="eyebrow">Research</p><h1>From plant strategies to planetary patterns</h1><p class="lead">Our research connects functional ecology, biogeography and global environmental data.</p></div></section>
<section class="section container"><div class="stack">
<article><span class="number">01</span><div><h2>Plant functional traits</h2><p>We investigate how traits reflect ecological strategies and how trait variation influences the structure and functioning of plant communities.</p></div></article>
<article><span class="number">02</span><div><h2>Biomes and vegetation–climate relationships</h2><p>We examine the climatic and ecological foundations of biomes, including transitions among forest types and broad thermal and moisture gradients.</p></div></article>
<article><span class="number">03</span><div><h2>Forest ecosystems and biodiversity</h2><p>We use forest inventories and species-level information to understand diversity patterns, species associations and ecosystem functioning.</p></div></article>
<article><span class="number">04</span><div><h2>Remote sensing and ecological big data</h2><p>We combine field observations, global databases, Earth observation and statistical modelling to study ecological processes across scales.</p></div></article>
<article><span class="number">05</span><div><h2>Global change ecology</h2><p>We explore how climate and other environmental changes alter plant distributions, community composition and ecosystem properties.</p></div></article>
</div></section>
